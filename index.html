SOSYAL Bot

SOSYAL Bot is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool. It runs only on its owner's own Windows computer and helps that single channel owner manage their own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API v3.

What it does

  • Creates short videos (script, voice-over, subtitles) on the owner's computer for the owner's channel.
  • Uploads videos only after the channel owner explicitly approves them, with the title, description, tags and thumbnail the owner approved.
  • Reads comments on the owner's own videos and prepares reply drafts; a reply is posted only after the owner approves it.
  • Reads the owner's channel and video statistics (views, likes, comment counts) to show which videos performed well.
  • Reads public lists of popular videos and search results to suggest content topics.

Nothing is uploaded, posted or replied to without the channel owner's explicit approval. The tool is not offered to the public and has no user accounts or servers; all data stays on the owner's computer.

Türkçe

Veriler yalnızca sahibinin bilgisayarında kalır.
